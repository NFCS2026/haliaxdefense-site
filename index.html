01  Capability

An intelligence layer for every operator

Haliax turns off-the-shelf smart glasses into a force multiplier. It listens, watches and translates, then tells the operator only what matters.

HEAR

Audio awareness

Picks out key words, alarms and sudden sounds in the noise, and alerts the operator to what they missed.

TRANSLATE

Live translation

Foreign speech and signage, translated and delivered straight to the operator's ear.

SEE

Scene awareness

Reads the environment through the glasses' camera and flags potential hazards before they reach you.

Operator control

Audio and video analytics switch on and off independently. The operator decides, every time.
